Samo Ramen Humanist is a humanist sans serif drawn with a slanted broad brush nib. Its warm, open letters have thin horizontals and thick verticals, a nod to Japanese signage and brush lettering, and soft, rounded bowls that keep text comfortable to read.

Contextual alternates for a, e, g, y, f and t change shape as you type, so repeated letters feel hand-made rather than stamped. It supports Western and Central European languages.
